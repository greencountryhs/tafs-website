Our Mission

Tulsa Area Forest School (TAFS) is a community-driven nature education program offering free, inclusive outdoor learning experiences for families across the Tulsa area. We gather 2–3 times each week in local parks and natural spaces, providing enriching, hands-on sessions tailored for all ages and abilities. At TAFS, we believe nature is for everyone. Our programs are open to all families—regardless of age, race, gender, ability, or background. Whether you're a local resident or visiting Tulsa, you are welcome here. Join us in fostering connection, curiosity, and community through nature. To learn more, follow our Facebook and Instagram pages.
