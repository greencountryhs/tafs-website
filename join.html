Join Tulsa Area Forest School

Ready to visit or become part of Tulsa Area Forest School? Start by completing our family intake and participation form. This helps us learn who is attending, understand any safety or support needs, and make sure every family has the information they need before joining us outdoors.

Complete the Family Intake Form

How Joining Works

1. Complete the form

Fill out the family intake and waiver form so we can welcome you thoughtfully.

2. 30-day trial

New families receive a 30-day trial period before dues are billed.

3. Semester dues

After the trial period, dues are $50 per family per semester.

4. Volunteer option

Families may offset dues through 2 approved volunteer hours per semester.

5. Scholarships

Scholarship assistance may be available for families who need it.

6. Stay and participate

Sessions are stay-and-participate. Parents and caregivers remain responsible for their own children.

Before You Attend

TFS is volunteer-run. Families should RSVP when attending sessions and are responsible for their own first aid preparedness. Outdoor play includes normal risks such as uneven ground, weather, mud, insects, sticks, rocks, water, tools, knives, hooks, and other outdoor hazards.

Families with custody, privacy, safety, allergy, medical, or accessibility concerns are encouraged to share that information in the form or contact leadership privately.

TFS includes families from many different backgrounds, beliefs, education styles, and life situations. This form is not a screening tool. It helps us better support the families who are seeking us out and understand what they are looking for in forest school.
